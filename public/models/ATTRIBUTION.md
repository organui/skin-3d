# Skin model attribution

`skin.glb` is an original, schematic block of skin generated by `scripts/prepare-model.ts` in this repository. It contains no scanned, traced, or third-party anatomy data.

The model is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is appreciated but not required:

> OrganUI Skin schematic model, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
